Ashby

Butler of the house. At your service.

How this works: Ashby is a small language model that runs inside your browser. The first time you press Download model, your browser fetches it once and keeps it in its cache. After that it runs entirely on your device: what you type is never sent to a server, and later visits load from the cache without downloading again.
